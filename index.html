// 05 — questions

          
Before you ask.

        

        

          

            How much will it cost?
            
It depends on the scope. Choose a rough budget in the form and we'll suggest what fits. You'll get a clear quote before any work starts.

          

          

            I only have a rough idea. Is that okay?
            
Yes. Most projects start as a few sentences, and we'll ask the right questions to fill in the rest.

          

          

            Do you take small jobs, like one PPT or one Excel sheet?
            
Yes. A single deck or spreadsheet gets the same care as a full app.

          

          

            Can you take over or redesign something that already exists?
            
Yes. Send us a link to your current app, site or file in the form and tell us what isn't working.

          

          

            Who owns the final product?
            
You do. Source files, code and accounts are handed over to you at the end of the project.
